# About This Fan Translation Project

This is an unofficial, non-commercial fan project dedicated to translating old, out-of-print French *Star Wars* roleplaying adventures into English.

These adventures were published in French gaming magazines and represent a fascinating little corner of *Star Wars* history. I wanted to make them accessible to English-speaking fans and preserve them for future players and gamemasters.

I'm including the original illustrations and creator credits wherever possible because they're part of the original work, and I think they deserve to be preserved alongside the text.

This is a hobby project. I don't make any money from it, and I'm not claiming ownership of the original stories, illustrations, or *Star Wars* intellectual property. All original material belongs to its respective rights holders.

*Star Wars* is owned by Lucasfilm and its associated rights holders, including Disney. This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Lucasfilm, Disney, or any official *Star Wars* organization.

I've done my best to credit the original writers, illustrators, and other contributors. If you're one of those creators, I'd love to hear from you! Corrections, additional historical information, and behind-the-scenes stories are all welcome.

If you're a rights holder or authorized representative with concerns about anything on this site, please get in touch. I'm happy to discuss things.

# Backstab Magazine Adventures In English
Roughly translated by darthbanjo in 2023 (and 2025 because apparently I forget to finish things) from *Backstab Magazine*, originally published in French. Backstab was a French roleplaying magazine published from 1996 to 2005 by FC Publications. Some Star Wars roleplaying articles and adventures were printed in the magazine.

# Descartes Supplement Adventures In English
Roughly translated by darthbanjo in 2026 from *Descartes: Le Supplément*, originally published in French. Descartes was a French roleplaying magazine published from from 1992 to 1995 by Jeux Descartes. Some Star Wars roleplaying articles and adventures were printed in the magazine.
